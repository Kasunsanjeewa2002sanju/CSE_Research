## 1. Conceptual Framework & Semantics

The Scenario Engine operationalizes conditional counterfactual reasoning on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) All Share Price Index (ASPI). It evaluates how a newly structured political or macroeconomic news item alters forward equity return expectations across multiple trading horizons ($h \in \{1, 2, 5, 10\}$ trading days).

### Core Definitions

1. **Scenario TRUE ($S_{\text{TRUE}}$) — The Event Occurs:**
   - The news event described is assumed to materialize.
   - The feature vector $X_{\text{TRUE}}$ is constructed with active event indicators:
     - Electoral stage one-hot encodings (`stage_*`) and election type encodings (`type_*`) corresponding to the structured news extraction from Stage 10.
     - Event proximity variables: `days_to_next_known_event = 0`, `days_since_last_stage = 0`, and active window indicators (`in_window_0_2 = 1`, `in_window_0_7 = 1`, etc.).
     - Historical event-study statistics (`prior_mean_car`, `prior_median_car`, `prior_n`) queried strictly as of the news date ($t$) using Stage 4 `prior_event_stats` (zero lookahead).
   - The frozen multi-horizon machine learning model is evaluated on $X_{\text{TRUE}}$ to produce:
     $$\hat{y}_{\text{ret, TRUE}}^{(h)}, \quad P(\text{Up})_{\text{TRUE}}^{(h)}, \quad \text{ASPI}_{\text{TRUE}}^{(h)}$$

2. **Scenario FALSE ($S_{\text{FALSE}}$) — The Event Does Not Occur:**
   - The counterfactual state where no political event takes place.
   - The feature vector $X_{\text{FALSE}}$ is constructed with all event indicators set to inert baseline values:
     - `stage_none = 1`, all other stages $= 0$.
     - `type_none = 1`, all other types $= 0$.
     - `days_to_next_known_event = -1`, `days_since_last_stage = -1`.
     - `in_window_0_2 = 0`, `in_window_0_7 = 0`, `in_window_0_15 = 0`, `in_window_0_30 = 0`.
     - `prior_mean_car = 0.0`, `prior_median_car = 0.0`, `prior_n = 0`.
   - All core market technicals, lags, volatility, and turnover features are kept strictly identical to $X_{\text{TRUE}}$.
   - The exact same frozen model is evaluated on $X_{\text{FALSE}}$ to produce:
     $$\hat{y}_{\text{ret, FALSE}}^{(h)}, \quad P(\text{Up})_{\text{FALSE}}^{(h)}, \quad \text{ASPI}_{\text{FALSE}}^{(h)}$$

3. **Mandatory Non-Claim Disclosure (No Simulated Reversals):**
   > [!IMPORTANT]
   > Our historical dataset (1994–2025) contains official verified records of scheduled elections, proclamations, and macro shocks, but **zero empirical examples of retracted, denied, or verified "fake news" announcements followed by market corrections**.
   > Therefore, **no hand-made panic-then-reversal or rumor-collapse trajectories are claimed, assumed, or simulated**.
   > Scenario FALSE strictly represents the unperturbed status-quo baseline forecast in the absence of an event shock.

4. **Net Impact ($\Delta$):**
   - **Return Impact:** $\Delta y_{\text{ret}}^{(h)} = \hat{y}_{\text{ret, TRUE}}^{(h)} - \hat{y}_{\text{ret, FALSE}}^{(h)}$
   - **Index Level Impact:** $\Delta \text{ASPI}^{(h)} = \text{ASPI}_{\text{TRUE}}^{(h)} - \text{ASPI}_{\text{FALSE}}^{(h)}$
   - **Directional Probability Impact:** $\Delta P(\text{Up})^{(h)} = P(\text{Up})_{\text{TRUE}}^{(h)} - P(\text{Up})_{\text{FALSE}}^{(h)}$

## 2. Multi-Horizon Forecasting & Sanity Bounds

### Mathematical Grounding (No Hand-Made Price Formulas)
To comply strictly with the research rule prohibiting ad-hoc price trajectories:
- Predictions for horizons $h \in \{1, 2, 5, 10\}$ trading days are produced directly by independent frozen CatBoost decision tree regressors trained to forecast forward log return:
  $$y_{\text{ret}}^{(h)} = \ln\left(\frac{\text{ASPI}_{t+h}}{\text{ASPI}_t}\right)$$
- Expected index level is reconstructed directly via:
  $$\hat{\text{ASPI}}_{t+h} = \text{ASPI}_t \cdot \exp\left(\hat{y}_{\text{ret}}^{(h)}\right)$$
- Probability of an upward market move $P(\text{Up})^{(h)} = P(y_{\text{ret}}^{(h)} > 0)$ is generated by a frozen classifier and calibrated via Platt scaling (logistic regression on inner-validation margins).

### Magnitude Cap via Historical Quantiles
To prevent explosive unphysical forecasts during severe covariate shifts, predicted returns are bounded by empirical 99.5th percentiles of historical absolute returns:
$$M_{\text{cap}}^{(h)} = \text{Quantile}_{99.5\%}(|y_{\text{ret}}^{(h)}|)$$
- $h = 1$ trading day: $\pm 4.97\%$
- $h = 2$ trading days: $\pm 7.91\%$
- $h = 5$ trading days: $\pm 12.48\%$
- $h = 10$ trading days: $\pm 17.09\%$
Any raw model forecast exceeding $M_{\text{cap}}^{(h)}$ is clipped at the cap, and the output dictionary sets `is_capped = True`.

## 3. Split-Conformal Prediction Intervals

Rather than assuming parametric Gaussian error distributions (which fail under emerging market fat tails and volatility clustering), prediction intervals are constructed using **inductive split-conformal prediction** from out-of-sample walk-forward validation residuals (Phases P1–P5, 2012–2025):

Let $\mathcal{R}^{(h)} = \{|y_{t,h} - \hat{y}_{t,h}|\}$ be the empirical set of out-of-sample absolute residuals.
For a target coverage level $1 - \alpha \in \{80\%, 95\%\}$:
$$q_{1-\alpha}^{(h)} = \text{Quantile}_{1-\alpha}(\mathcal{R}^{(h)})$$

### Event-Adaptive Residual Stratification
- **Scenario TRUE:** Uses residuals computed strictly over **event-window periods** ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{event}}^{(h)}$). Because political events induce heightened market volatility, the conformal quantile automatically expands to provide valid coverage.
- **Scenario FALSE:** Uses residuals computed over **non-event periods** ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{non-event}}^{(h)}$), reflecting baseline market noise.

The resulting prediction interval for forward ASPI level is:
$$\left[\text{ASPI}_t \cdot \exp\left(\hat{y} - q_{1-\alpha}\right), \; \text{ASPI}_t \cdot \exp\left(\hat{y} + q_{1-\alpha}\right)\right]$$

## 4. Historical Evidence Block & Strength Scoring

The historical evidence block retrieves empirical event study metrics from Stage 4 (`results/tables/event_study_by_type_stage.csv` and `event_study_by_stage.csv`).

### Fields Extracted:
- `matching_category`: Mapped event category.
- `matching_stage`: Mapped election cycle stage.
- `historical_sample_size (n)`: Number of completed historical events.
- `mean_car`: Cumulative Abnormal Return over $[0, +7]$ trading days.
- `median_car`: Median Cumulative Abnormal Return.
- `t_statistic`: Brown-Warner single-index test statistic.
- `t_pvalue`: Parametric significance.
- `permutation_pvalue`: 10,000-draw non-event placebo permutation test $p$-value.
- `small_sample_warning`: True if $n < 5$.

### Documented Rule for `evidence_strength`:
To ensure transparency and eliminate subjective or made-up confidence metrics:
1. **`strong`**: $n \ge 10$ AND $p < 0.05$ (Robust sample with statistically significant abnormal return).
2. **`moderate`**: ($n \ge 5$ AND $p < 0.10$), OR ($n \ge 15$ with consistent sign and $p \in [0.10, 0.20]$).
3. **`weak`**: $n < 5$ (small sample warning applies), OR $p \ge 0.20$, OR no direct historical match ($n = 0$).

## 5. Input Freshness & Staleness Rules

- The news publication date is evaluated against the historical trading calendar:
  - If date $\le \text{2025-12-31}$ (the final date in the research dataset), `current_aspi_level` is retrieved as the closing ASPI on or immediately preceding that date.
  - If date $> \text{2025-12-31}$ and the caller does not supply a live `current_aspi_level`, the engine sets `status = "STALE"` and refuses or explicitly flags that the index level reflects the historical cutoff.
